Management Brief — Sample Dataset

Business context:
The sales CSV contains six months of sample revenue, order volume, and returns for two regions/products. North sells Widget A; South sells Widget B. These are fictional demonstration records, not real business results.

Questions for the AI team:
1. Compare monthly revenue patterns by region/product.
2. Check whether returns are a potential operational concern.
3. Recommend practical follow-up analysis without assuming causes that are not present in the data.

Constraints:
Do not claim a trend proves causation. Call out the small sample size and the lack of cost, customer, and reason-for-return fields.
